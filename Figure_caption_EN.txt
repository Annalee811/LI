Figure. Joint ConvLSTM-GAT module, structural decoding and independent-position prediction.

(A) Dice for new and existing roots from a frame-only negative control, a frozen historical V66 prior, the model without the joint ConvLSTM-GAT module, and the Full model. Existing roots are the pixel-level union of persistent and extending segments. Three newly trained joint-ablation runs were compared with the original three matched Full runs; training settings and checkpoint selection were retained. The historical V66 result is one reference, not three seed replicates. Error bars denote sample optimization SD, not biological variability. Scores cover 83,478 common held-out typed-root pixels within B3; background is not evaluated.

(B) The ConvLSTM-GAT module is treated as one component. Its effect is Full minus the model with both recurrent memory and both GAT layers removed. Gate is Full minus the model without learned attention-edge identity gating. Both comparisons are conditional on their specified component boundaries, not a sequence of additions; when GAT is removed its attention-edge gating also has no aggregation path, although the auxiliary connection head is retained. Removing the joint module retains the frame CNN, node encoder, frozen upstream priors, historical features and auxiliary heads. It therefore does not remove all temporal information. Axis and Junction denote paired increments from the raw head to axis decoding, and from axis decoding to axis-plus-junction decoding. Those two decoder rows use a separate whole-field inference context and must not be summed with neural-module effects. Values are changes in two-class macro Dice (percentage points), with paired optimization SD across three seeds.

(C) The same 256 x 256-pixel original RGB field in Q1-2-B3 at T1-T4, overlaid with unchanged predictions from the Full raw neural head, seed identifier 20261123. Gold: new roots; blue: existing roots; gray: baseline roots without a preceding observation and without an assigned temporal class. The bar is 50 native pixels. These predictions do not include the external axis/junction decoder. No human temporal ground truth is available at this position; independent accuracy is not estimated. There was prior unlabeled image exposure and a prepared feature adapter with documented approximations, so this is not prospective blind-image validation.

Reproducibility note. The three new ablation runs use 100 full-graph optimization updates each, sampling up to 8192 training pixels per update; this is not 100 full-data epochs. The original three-class training objective and validation macro-Dice checkpoint criterion are unchanged to match the existing Full comparator. Two-class metrics are recalculated by merging truth and prediction categories before scoring, not by averaging old category metrics. This is not new two-class-objective training. Current results concern conditional temporal classification, not full-background end-to-end segmentation or proof of correct root-instance attribution. Existing image files, labels, model weights and prior results were preserved.
