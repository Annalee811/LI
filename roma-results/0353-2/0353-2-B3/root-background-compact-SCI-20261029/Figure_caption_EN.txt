Figure X. Four-period comparison of root-background segmentation by U-Net, DeepLabV3+, and SegFormer-B0. (A) Full-field views of the original RGB images, reference annotations, and segmentation results at T1-T4. Yellow boxes indicate the matched 256 x 256-pixel regions enlarged in (B). (B) Local comparisons of the original RGB images, reference annotations, and predictions from the three models. Root foreground pixels retain their original RGB colors, whereas the removed background is shown in white. The same local window is used across periods and methods. Scale bars: 500 pixels in (A) and 50 pixels in (B).
