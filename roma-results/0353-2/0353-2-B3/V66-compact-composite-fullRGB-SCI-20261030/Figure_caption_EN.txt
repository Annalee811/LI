Figure X. Training dynamics and temporal root classification performance of U-Net, DeepLabV3+, and SegFormer-B0. (A) Actual training and validation losses over 100 epochs, with colors identifying models and solid/dashed lines indicating training/validation. (B) Confusion matrices pooled over the same 468,203 reference root pixels in T2–T4. Rows denote reference classes and columns denote predictions. Cells show counts and row percentages; the Missed column retains undetected reference roots. (C) Four-period full-field comparisons showing the manual temporal labels overlaid on the original RGB images, the same manual labels on white backgrounds, and the predictions of U-Net, DeepLabV3+, and SegFormer-B0 on white backgrounds. Dashed boxes mark the matched 384 × 384-pixel regions enlarged in (D), where the reference labels and model predictions are overlaid on the original RGB images. Purple, green, and yellow indicate persistent, new, and extending roots, respectively; blue indicates the T1 baseline. T1 is displayed but excluded from the three-class temporal metrics. Full fields are 2541 × 1108 pixels.
