Figure. Two-class temporal root classification: baseline performance, component effects and independent-sample predictions.

(A) Class-specific Dice for the frame-only negative control, the frozen V66 prior, the Full model without GAT and the Full model. New roots retain the original newly emerged category; existing roots are the union of persistent and extending segments. Categories were merged at the pixel level before recomputing scores. Points show means with sample standard deviations across three optimization seeds, except for the single frozen V66 reference. Evaluation uses the common 83,478 annotated root pixels in the internal B3 held-out partition (70,907 existing and 12,571 new); background is excluded. The frame-only variant collapses to the existing class and is a negative control, rather than a tuned strong baseline. The V66 reference has a historical training protocol.

(B) Paired changes in two-class macro Dice, in percentage points. The first three rows are Full minus the corresponding ablated model, evaluated in the partition-isolated internal setting. The last two rows show axis decoding minus the raw head and axis-plus-junction decoding minus axis decoding, respectively, in a separate paired whole-field inference setting. Points and whiskers represent means and sample standard deviations across three matched optimization seeds. The two blocks use different reference contexts and should not be summed or interpreted as one continuous experiment. The ConvLSTM ablation does not show a positive mean contribution in this comparison.

(C) A single 256 x 256-pixel field from Q1-2-B3, shown at the same coordinates (x = 208, y = 1456) across T1-T4. Predictions from the retrained Full raw neural head (seed identifier 20261123) are superimposed on original RGB images. Gold denotes new roots and blue denotes existing roots. T1 is a baseline without a preceding observation; its root pixels are gray and have no assigned temporal class. The white scale bar is 50 native pixels. This is an inference example, not a measured independent accuracy result: human temporal ground truth is unavailable for this position. The model uses the prepared feature adapter, which has documented approximations; there was prior exposure to unlabeled images. These images therefore do not establish performance on prospectively unseen RGB images. They do not include the external axis-and-junction decoding stage.

Protocol note. This figure recomputes two-class scores from existing predictions; it does not retrain the network with a two-class objective. The original checkpoints were selected using the three-class validation criterion. The three seeds describe optimization variability, not independent biological replication. Grouping persistent and extending segments changes the classification taxonomy and is not evidence that root-instance attribution is solved. No inference, registration or training was rerun to create this compact figure.

中文图注
图：两类根段时序分类的基线性能、组件效应与独立样本预测。A为新生根和既有根的逐类Dice，既有根由持续根与延伸根的像素级并集合并后重新计算；评估限定于B3内部留出分区的已标注根像素，不含背景。B为宏平均Dice的配对变化：上部为完整模型减去对应消融模型，下部为结构解码各阶段相对前一阶段的增量；两个部分使用不同的比较基准，不能相加。C为Q1-2-B3同一局部视野T1—T4的原始RGB叠加预测，金色表示新生根，蓝色表示既有根；T1灰色为未赋予时序类别的基线根段。该样本缺少人工时序真值，因此不报告独立准确率。

结果解读
完整模型的新生根和既有根Dice分别为81.63%和96.79%，两类宏平均Dice为89.21%；冻结V66参考的两类宏平均Dice同为约89.21%，现有结果不支持完整神经模型显著优于该历史参考的结论。模块配对差值较小，其中LSTM的平均效应为负；结构解码中的根轴约束在其独立配对设置下带来约2.885个百分点的宏平均Dice提升，交叉连接约束随后增加约0.406个百分点。独立样本图展示跨时期的分类预测形态，泛化准确性仍需新增人工真值验证。
