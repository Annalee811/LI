Figure. Temporal baselines, structural ablation, and temporal root classification on minirhizotron images.

(A) Macro Dice for the historical frame-only control and V66 U-Net pairwise temporal baseline, the matched neural-only control, and the jointly trained full model. The neural-only control retains the ConvLSTM–GAT network, identity gate, and common auxiliary supervision with learned axis and junction refinement and their additional consistency losses disabled. (B) Differences in macro Dice between the full model and the matched ablations. Positive values favor the full model. Each difference is computed within the same optimization seed. (C) Class-specific precision, recall, Dice, and intersection over union (IoU) for newly observed roots, and Dice and IoU for existing roots. Open circles represent the frozen V66 baseline, and filled squares represent the full model. Existing roots include persistent segments and elongation of existing root axes. Quantitative scores in A–C use the same 83,478 annotated root pixels in the internal B3 holdout across T2–T4 (12,571 new, 70,907 existing). The V66 result is a frozen historical reference with one prediction set. Retrained configurations are summarized over three optimization seeds. Error bars represent sample standard deviations. (D) Four dates at the same registered field in position 0353-2-B3. Columns show original RGB, human annotation, V66 U-Net, and the full joint model. The joint-model overlays are saved whole-field predictions from the checkpoint selected by validation macro Dice, seed 20261125. Gold denotes newly observed roots, blue denotes existing roots, and gray denotes T1 baseline roots without temporal classification. The scale bar represents 100 native image pixels.

The internal evaluation is conditional on root foreground, with historical B3 exposure in the frozen upstream prior. It supports an internal comparison of temporal root classification. The montage depicts class labels, and the quantitative region used for evaluation is the partition-isolated internal holdout.

A比较历史单帧对照、原始U-Net时序基线（冻结V66）、同协议的神经网络对照及联合训练完整模型的两类宏平均Dice。神经网络对照保留ConvLSTM＋GAT、身份门控及共有辅助监督，关闭可学习的根轴和交叉细化及其额外一致性损失。B为完整模型减去各消融配置的宏平均Dice差，按随机种子配对计算，正值表示完整模型得分较高。C比较V66和完整模型的新生根精确率、召回率、Dice及IoU，以及既有根Dice和IoU。空心圆为V66，实心方块为完整模型。既有根包括持续根段及原有根轴的延伸部分。A至C使用B3内部留出分区T2–T4的同一批83,478个已标注根像素，其中新生根12,571个、既有根70,907个。冻结V66为单组历史参考，重新训练的配置报告三个优化随机种子的均值，误差棒表示样本标准差。D展示0353-2-B3四期同一配准局部视野的原始RGB、人工标注、V66预测和联合模型预测。联合模型叠加图来自按验证集宏平均Dice选定的权重（种子20261125）的已有整幅预测。金色、蓝色和灰色分别表示新生根、既有根与T1基线根段。标尺为100个原生图像像素。
