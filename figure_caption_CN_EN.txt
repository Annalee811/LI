English caption
Local candidate-axis ownership and temporal root-segment labels on original RGB. All four panels use identical registered coordinates x=1530:1770, y=870:1050 in T2 of 0353-2-B3 (240×180 source pixels). A, original RGB. B, archived V17 axis fragments within the common foreground domain of the two selected final candidates; open circles mark original path endpoints rather than manually introduced breaks. C, archived V18 candidate-axis ownership: display IDs R1 and R2 correspond to source candidate axes 327 and 388. Teal and terracotta distinguish these two local candidate axes only. D, archived V18 temporal labels on the same two axes: blue, persistent (P); amber/orange, newly emerged (N); purple/magenta, elongating (E). Source records classify R1 as a newly observed candidate root and R2 as a persistent root with a terminal extension. Gray retains existing uncertain crossing pixels rather than forcing confident axis or class colors. Display IDs do not establish independently verified biological root identities; source records retain unresolved-crossing flags for both complete paths. Ownership is taken from the existing single-valued axis_map, without painting two instance colors at one pixel. Candidate trajectory association does not fill missing root pixels or change RGB/root-mask geometry. The 50-pixel scale applies to all panels; no unverified physical calibration is introduced. This is a local illustration of existing V17-to-V18 geometry and temporal decoding, not new inference, independent validation or a population-level estimate of improvement.
