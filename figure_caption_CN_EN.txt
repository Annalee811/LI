English caption
Quantitative and local qualitative comparison of V66 and V90 temporal root classification. A, date-specific Dice for newly emerged, elongating and persistent roots at T2, T3 and T4. Dashed lines with open circles denote V66; solid lines with filled circles denote V90. Labels indicate percentages. Each score was recomputed from complete archived fields against common frozen reference B over typed reference-root pixels, excluding true background and ignored pixels while retaining missed roots as false negatives; scores are not restricted to the illustrated crop. T1 is baseline and excluded. Reference B contains no elongating-root pixels at T4, so the corresponding Dice points are marked n.a.; predicted false positives remain in the raw counts and original T2–T4 pooled evaluation. B, original RGB, human reference, V66 and V90 overlays at identical registered coordinates x=1000:1480, y=500:760 in 0353-2-B3 across T1–T4 (480×260 pixels). No whole-field panels are shown. Amber/orange, purple/magenta and blue denote newly emerged, elongating and persistent roots; white denotes T1 baseline and gray denotes ignored reference pixels. The 100-pixel scale bar applies to all local panels at identical magnification; no unverified physical calibration is introduced. The ROI was selected from reference morphology and class coverage, without ranking V90 improvement. Background RGB and original label geometry remain unchanged. Colors encode temporal classes rather than unique root-instance identity. V66 is the archived U-Net temporal output; V90 is the final learned, structurally decoded and manually reviewed workflow, not a neural-only output. Human reference B contains manual temporal assignments with pre-existing U-Net-assisted binary outline refinement rather than independent blinded anatomical ground truth. This is a within-development-position agreement comparison, not independent cross-tube validation or a controlled architecture-only ablation. No new training, inference, registration or annotation was performed.
