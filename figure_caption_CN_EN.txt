V66-to-V90 temporal segment comparison on two candidate root axes. All panels use identical registered coordinates x=957:1197, y=312:492 in T3 of 0353-2-B3 (240×180 source pixels). A, original RGB; B, archived V66 U-Net temporal class output; C, final archived V90 learned, structurally decoded and manually reviewed class output; D, corresponding single-valued V90 candidate-axis pixel ownership. Only two candidate axes are highlighted; all other RGB pixels remain unchanged. Blue, amber/orange and purple/magenta indicate persistent (P), newly emerged (N) and elongating (E) segments. Teal and terracotta in D identify candidate axes R1 (source ID271) and R2 (source ID192), not temporal states. R1 is newly emerged in both versions. The illustrated R2 contains persistent/new labels in V66 and persistent/extension labels in V90, illustrating temporal class consistency along one candidate axis while retaining a separate adjacent new candidate. Image contact does not establish independently validated parentage or lateral-root identity. Binary root-mask geometry is unchanged, and neither improved segmentation shape nor independently validated instance accuracy is claimed. V66 has no directly comparable instance-owner map in the released inputs; D therefore shows V90 ownership only. B and C compare genuine class outputs on the same pair of V90 candidate masks without inventing V66 root IDs. Candidate ownership was recovered by replaying the released V90 structural/review steps from archived neural features and pair logits. All four final class maps matched archived V90 pixel-for-pixel, without new training or a ConvLSTM/GAT forward pass. V90 includes pre-existing manual review and is not neural-only output. This selected example is qualitative rather than an independent cross-tube validation or an estimate of overall improvement. Scale bar, 50 pixels, common to all panels.
