Controlled evaluation of temporal modules and structural decoding for root classification

With frozen upstream segmentation features and V66 temporal priors, a matched optimization budget, and validation-based checkpoint selection, the full newly trained module configuration achieved an internal macro Dice of 63.71 ± 0.35%, compared with 62.56% for the frozen V66 reference (Fig. A). Dice scores were 97.28%, 81.63%, and 12.23% for persistent, newly observed, and extending roots, respectively, revealing substantial class imbalance in performance (Fig. B). Seed-matched differences between the full and ablated configurations were 0.76 ± 0.13, 0.30 ± 0.47, and 0.02 ± 0.11 percentage points for GAT, ConvLSTM, and learned identity gates, respectively (Fig. C). GAT produced a positive difference in all three optimization seeds, whereas the identity-gate effect was small and inconsistent. In a separate paired whole-field decoding experiment using identical neural outputs within each seed, macro Dice increased from 63.70 ± 0.34% for the raw head to 73.51 ± 0.50% with axis constraints and 76.71 ± 1.99% with axis and junction constraints (Fig. D). The largest class-specific change occurred for extending roots, whose Dice increased from 12.38 ± 1.18% to 45.71 ± 5.45%; newly observed roots increased from 81.48 ± 0.17% to 87.09 ± 0.59%, while persistent-root Dice remained approximately 97% (Fig. E). Final pairwise instance precision, recall, and F1 were 95.91 ± 0.45%, 49.77 ± 1.07%, and 65.52 ± 0.85%, respectively, and remained close to the axis-only values (Fig. F). Thus, improved temporal class agreement did not establish improved recovery of complete root identities, and fragmentation remained substantial. These results describe conditional internal B3 experiments; optimization-seed variation does not constitute biological replication. The current-frame classifier collapsed to persistent-only predictions under this budget and should be interpreted as a negative control rather than a fully tuned single-frame benchmark. Independent cross-tube accuracy remains unmeasured because human temporal ground truth is unavailable.

Figure X. Controlled evaluation of neural modules and paired structural decoding for temporal root classification. (A) Internal macro Dice of a frozen V66 reference and five newly trained configurations, evaluated on common eligible root pixels. Each trained configuration used three fixed optimization seeds, 100 full-graph updates, and 8,192 sampled training root pixels per update. The historical V66 reference was not retrained. (B) Mean class Dice for persistent, newly observed (New), and extending roots. Cell shading spans 0–100%; printed values are means. (C) Seed-matched differences in macro Dice between the full configuration and each module ablation (full minus ablated; pp, percentage points). Positive values favor retaining the specified module. Removing ConvLSTM retains other temporal features and graph edges; removing learned identity gates retains GAT attention and the fixed geometric prior. (D) Macro Dice at successive structural decoding stages using the same whole-field neural outputs within each seed. Thin gray lines connect matched seeds. (E) Corresponding class-specific Dice. (F) Pairwise instance precision, recall, and F1 after axis-only or axis-plus-junction decoding, evaluated on 69,519 eligible pixels with reference root identities. These instance metrics differ from graph-edge identity-link F1. Points and error bars represent means and sample standard deviations over optimization seeds (n = 3), not confidence intervals or biological replicates. Small black points in A and C denote individual seeds; the frozen V66 reference is a single observation. Internal temporal class scores pool T2–T4 eligible typed root pixels (83,478 pixels: 69,879 persistent, 12,571 newly observed, and 1,028 extending), exclude background, and condition on a fixed foreground. The two rows use distinct inference contexts and must not be combined into one protocol. Upstream features and priors had historical B3 exposure; results are internal reference agreement rather than independent generalization estimates. Predictions are available for three additional tubes (seven positions, 26 frames), but no independent Dice or IoU is reported because human temporal ground truth is unavailable.
